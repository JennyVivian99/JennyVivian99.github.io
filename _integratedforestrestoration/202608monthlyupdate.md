# 🌴🌳Monthly update: August 2026🪴🌽
It is always amazing how scientists seek answers through experiments, complex analyses, deep studies... All done to understand the natural phenomena that originated from (fortunate) casual combinations. Maybe we have to flip our perspective when things don't go as planned: well-planned paths may combine with intuition, bold ideas, and risky roads.
This month, let's try to embrace the uncertainty and the changes of perspective, to discover that there is beauty in it, the blessing of leaving all doors open...
