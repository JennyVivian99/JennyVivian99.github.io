# 🌴🌳Monthly update: September 2026🪴🌽
As the flowers shine when the Sun sheds its light on them, our efforts become known when the interest of other people falls into them.
Long-term and strong efforts to make our planet healthy and thriving must not remain pieces of knowledge in aseptic repositories and libraries. When we discover system dynamics, ecosystem traits and related risks of degradation, we should (or we must!) act- The academic knowledge is not built to be just ink on an A4 sheet. It is the product of a collective effort toward a common goal: the enhancement of the health of nature.
