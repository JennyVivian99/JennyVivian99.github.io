# 🌲🌱Monthly update: November 2026❄️🔥
The sun is still warm, but shivering in the shade is getting quite common. Autumn is arriving in the Northern Hemisphere, while in the Southern Hemisphere temperatures keep rising.
As I cross the street, I see leaves falling, being decomposed by invisible essential workers. The scent of Autumn is travelling through a sharp wind coming from the North. 
The nutrient cycle is so important and evident in front of us. Yet, there is still much we have to learn about it...
This month, I propose that you reflect on this topic, and also on the mechanisms that may help us to understand dynamics like this in the ecosystem.
